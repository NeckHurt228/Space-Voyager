# Benchmarks: Combatful, ArmoredPOVs, AirHard

Data pulled Nov 2026 with vidIQ (top ~50 Shorts per channel, plus 7 second-by-second
watches). AirHard's videos were not watched frame by frame; its numbers come from
titles and stats only.

## Channel profiles

| | Combatful | ArmoredPOVs | AirHard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical length | 20–50s | 5–15s | 5–18s |
| Content | Military clips told as stories: reunions, wholesome moments, comedy, conflict | Military and airsoft fails, played as comedy | Airsoft POV: fails, clever plays, drama between players |
| Biggest hit | Graduation surprise, 72.9M | "Soldier Breaks Physics", 26.6M | "Homeowner Thinks Airsoft Gun Is Real", 66.4M |
| Title pattern | Statement + "..." | "When [Role] [does X].. #military #usa" | "When Airsofter [does X]…" |
| Likes / views | 2–5% (emotional videos highest) | about 0.8–1.4% | about 0.5–1.8% |
| How results spread | Many 2–70M videos | Extreme: top 3 ≈ 71M, most others 50–90K | Many 5–66M videos |

**Key lesson (ArmoredPOVs):** the same template gets 26M on one video and 60K on the next.
The clip choice and title decide which; the edit multiplies it.

## Second-by-second patterns from the hits

**When Soldier Breaks Physics (7s):** 0:00 `SOLDIER: *THROWS GRENADE*` + tense drone →
0:01 `*WAITING..*` → 0:02 camera whips round, the grenade is inside the pit → 0:03 a pi symbol and maths formulas
pop up, the picture desaturates, a meme voice says "Oh my god, what the…" → 0:05 `SOLDIER: DAMN..`,
a laugh clip, **cut mid-reaction**. Payoff at about 40%.

**When C-RAM Goes Too Far (8s):** 0:00 the gun slews up, `C-RAM: *LOCKING AT TARGET*` → 0:01 cut
to a thermal view of an airliner, `*CIVILIAN PLANE*`, distorted "NOOO" → 0:03 B&W, `*THINKS ABOUT IT..*`
→ 0:04 the gun lowers, `*MAYBE NEXT TIME*` → cartoon laugh. **All original audio is replaced.**

**When Airsofter Brings Wrong Gun (7s):** 0:00 sprint, `AIRSOFTER: *RUSHING ENEMY*` over a goofy yodel
(the music hints it isn't serious) → 0:02 B&W + bass hit + red `*SH*T WRONG GUN..*` → 0:05 a tiny toy
pistol *pop* → `*ENEMY IS COOKED..*`, hard cut. Text colour: yellow for setup → red for punchline.

**Sweetest Graduation Ceremony (48s):** 0:00 the principal says "we have a small problem…", header
`*AT GRADUATION CEREMONY*`, `PRINCIPAL` tag, word-by-word captions → 0:03 punch-in on the student,
`*MISSING HIS DIPLOMA*` → `*DISSAPOINTED*` → 0:18 a voice over the speakers, a soldier stands up in the crowd
`*SISTER UNCOVERS*` → piano builds from 0:28 → `*REUNION HUG*`. Discomfort first, then the payoff.

**Royal Guard Breaks Character (41s):** 30s of an escalating made-up story with synced captions →
0:31 `*TRYING NOT TO LAUGH*` (**a small payoff that signals the big one**) → 0:35 two
quick punch-ins + victory-scream SFX → `*VICTORY WALK*`. The title gives away the ending; viewers stay
to see the exact moment it happens.

**US Army Grenade Training (35s):** POV, `SOLDIER: *HANDS GRENADE*`, pin-pull clicks, spoken
countdown captions `ONE / TWO / THREE` → explosion at 0:16 → a second throw repeats the tension → explosion
at 0:33 → ends on "Good…". The countdown keeps people watching.

## Common technical choices across all hits
- 9:16; horizontal sources centred with **blurred copies of the frame filling the top and bottom**.
- Captions centred in the lower-middle area. Coloured `ROLE:` tag (green / cyan / yellow box) and
  bold white `*ACTION*` text with a black outline.
- Watermark `@handle` at the bottom centre, all the way through.
- **None of the 7 watched hits loop seamlessly.** They all hard-cut on the reaction.
- Comedy: original audio is often replaced entirely (drone → meme voice → laugh clip).
- Story: original audio is kept and soft music is held back until the reveal.

## Comment-argument signal
High comment counts come from clips where viewers take sides: "Soldier Stands Up To Karen" 19.2K,
"Kid Burning His Patch" 11.5K, "Military Officer Provoked At Gas Station" 10K, "Takes The Game Too
Seriously" 8K, "Why Kids Shouldn't Play Airsoft" 7.8K. Pure feel-good clips get likes, not comments.
